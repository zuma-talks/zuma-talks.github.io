Sep 15 at ZJU
  • Speaker: Dingxin Zhang 张鼎新 (SIMIS,Shanghai)
  • Title: Cohomological Divisibility of Exponential Sums
  • Using the theory of the sheaf-theoretic Mellin transform of Gabber–Loeser, we prove that the Adolphson–Sperber p-adic bound for exponential sums holds for every individual ℓ-adic Frobenius eigenvalue attached to an exponential sum over F_q, uniformly for all ℓ not dividing q.
